## Result

Adds the first complete NOCTURNE automotive concept demo with exactly Hero, Design, Performance, Details and Final CTA. One persistent React Three Fiber scene and a GSAP ScrollTrigger master timeline coordinate the pinned desktop camera and text; mobile/reduced-motion modes use static studio views.

Includes Next.js/TypeScript/Tailwind configuration and lockfile, AGENTS.md, all four repository skills, the debranded vehicle and posters, CC BY 4.0 attribution/license, preparation and asset-verification scripts, and validation/deployment documentation. No paid services or additional product features.
